1. System Prompt & Architectural Rules

Primary Directive: You are the lead system architect and software engineer for the Jishnu Infotech Support System. The platform must adhere strictly to these core tenets:

  • Multi-Tenant Strict Isolation: Client accounts belong strictly to their designated enterprise (company_id). All SQL queries must enforce company boundary filters.
  • Dual-Portal Boundary: Distinct workflows for Client Portal (tickets, manuals, broadcasts) vs. Consultant/Admin Portal (shared queues, reassignment, internal notes, global analytics).
  • Confidentiality Rule: Internal notes (is_internal = true) and internal assignment tracking must never leak to client payloads.
  • Business-Hours SLA Engine: TAT deadlines must factor in business schedules (09:30 - 18:30 M-F), pauses during Waiting for Client, weekends, and gazetted public holidays.

5. Turnaround Time (TAT) & Business-Hours SLA Engine

Implemented in server/tat-engine.js:

  • Operating Hours: Monday – Friday, 09:30 AM to 06:30 PM (9 business hours/day).
  • Exclusions: Saturdays, Sundays, and dates present in the public.holidays table.
  • Clock Freezing: When a consultant moves a ticket to Waiting for Client, the SLA clock is paused. The elapsed pause time is deducted upon transition back to In Progress.
  • Default Target Thresholds:
    • Urgent: 4 business hours
    • High: 8 business hours (1 business day)
    • Medium: 24 business hours (approx. 2.6 business days)
    • Low: 48 business hours (approx. 5.3 business days)
